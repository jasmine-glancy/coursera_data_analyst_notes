## Summary

A colleague test involves showing a presentation to coworkers who are unfamiliar with the subject matter in order to get their feedback. The colleague test helps you prepare for potential stakeholder questions and shows you what parts of your presentation may be unclear. If your stakeholder has a concern about a problem you didn’t realize, you can acknowledge the objection and promise to take steps to investigate further. To consider data limitations, think about the context, and understand the strengths and weaknesses of tools. When you receive an objection with merit, acknowledging its validity, follow up with details, and take steps to investigate the matter further.

If, during your colleague test, a coworker says they don't understand one of your graphs, you can elaborate on the data from the graph and redesign it for the next presentation.
