## Summary

Data Design is the way you organize your information. Analytical skills are qualities and characteristics associated with solving problems using facts. A technical mindset involves breaking processes down into smaller steps and working with them in an orderly, logical way. Data strategy involves managing processes and tools used in data analysis.
