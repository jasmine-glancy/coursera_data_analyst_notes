## Summary

There are six phases to the data life cycle (not to be confused with the data analysis process). The data life cycle stages in order are plan, capture, manage, analyze, archive, and destroy. The planning phase is when the data teams decide what kind of data is needed and who will be responsible for it. 

During the manage phase of the data life cycle, the data team considers how and where the data will be stored, what tools are required to safeguard it, and how to maintain it properly. A database is a computer system in which organizations store their data.
