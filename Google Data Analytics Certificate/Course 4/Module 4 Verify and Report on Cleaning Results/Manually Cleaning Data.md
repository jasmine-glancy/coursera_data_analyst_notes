## Summary

Verifying your data ensures it is reliable and well-cleaned, protecting a business from costly mistakes in analysis. To verify data, analysts recheck the data-cleaning effort, manually fix errors, and consider whether the data is credible and appropriate for the project. The `COUNTA` function enables data professionals to count the total number of spreadsheet values within a specified range. Changelogs are files containing chronologically ordered lists of modifications made to a project. They’re organized by version and include the data followed by a list of added, improved, and removed features.
