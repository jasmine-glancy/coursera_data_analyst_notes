## Summary

Documenting the evolution of a dataset makes it possible to recover data-cleaning errors, inform other users of changes, and determine the quality of the data. After a change to a query is submitted, all team members will be able to access the new query when they sync to the most up-to-date version control system. Syncing ensures the most up-to-date version of the query is the one being accessed. 

A changelog typically includes the component that changed and the reason why; a description of the change, and the date. It also includes the people who made and approved the change and the version number. A code review is an informal review of a change. Once the reviewer approves the change, the analyst submits the updated version to the company's version control system. This is called a code commit. Your team may keep pipelines in a version control system, which enables them to track the evolution of the pipelines over time.

To take a “big-picture view” during the data verification process, you confirm the cleaned data is capable of solving the original business problem and meeting the project objectives.
