## Take Action with Data

| Concept                 | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ask**                 | - Define the problem that needs to be solved. <br>- Zoom out and look at the whole part of the process<br>- What problem are you trying to solve?<br>- Make sure you fully understand the stakeholder's expectations<br>- Focus on the actual problem and avoid any distractions<br>- Collaborate with stakeholders and keep an open line of communication<br>- Take a step back and see the whole situation in context<br><br>**QUESTIONS TO ASK YOURSELF IN THIS STEP:** <br>1. What are my stakeholders saying their problems are?<br>2. Now that I've identified the issues, how can I help the stakeholders resolve their questions? |
| **Prepare**             | - Decide what data you need to collect in order to answer your questions and how to organize it so that it is useful. <br>- You might use your business task to decide:<br>&emsp;• What metrics to measure<br>&emsp;• Locate data in your database<br>&emsp;• Create security measures to protect that data<br><br>**QUESTIONS TO ASK YOURSELF IN THIS STEP:** <br>1. What do I need to figure out how to solve this problem?<br>2. What research do I need to do?                                                                                                                                                                        |

What data errors or inaccuracies might get in the way of my getting the best possible answer to the problem I am trying to solve?<br>2. How can I clean my data so the information I have is more consistent?                           |
| **Analyze**             | - At this stage, you might sort and format your data to make it easier to: <br>&emsp;• Perform Calculations<br>&emsp;• Combine data from multiple sources<br>&emsp;• Create tables with your results<br><br>**QUESTIONS TO ASK YOURSELF IN THIS STEP:** <br>1. What story is my data telling me?<br>2. How will my data help me solve this problem?<br>3. Who needs my company's product or service? What type of person is most likely to use it?                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Share**               | - This is your chance to show the stakeholders you have solved their problem and how you got there. Sharing will certainly help your team <br>&emsp;• Make better decisions<br>&emsp;• Make more informed decisions<br>&emsp;• Lead to stronger outcomes<br>&emsp;• Successfully communicate your findings<br><br>**QUESTIONS TO ASK YOURSELF IN THIS STEP:** <br>1. How can I make what I present to the stakeholders engaging and easy to understand?<br>2. What would help me understand this if I were the listener                                                                                                                   |

## Summary

When you get off track, the data analysis process can reel you back in. If a data analyst helps their employer choose the best place to advertise, they are researching how to reach their target audience. 

The prepare step includes collecting data for analysis. When data professionals clean data, they eliminate inaccuracies by transforming the data into a more useful format and removing outliers. Data cleaning takes place during the process step of the data analysis process. During the share step of the data analysis process, data professionals summarize results, often using visuals to help prepare understand data insights.
