#  Data Analysis Project

## Data Analyst: Jasmine Glancy

## Client/Sponsor: Whiskers & Wags Veterinary Urgent Care
A fictional veterinary urgent care facility. 

## Purpose: 

*Write a brief description of why this project is happening below. Why is this project happening? What are the goals?*

The goal of this project is to analyze veterinary visits across a span of 6 months to the Urgent Care in Raleigh to determine the most likely need for new staff members. The project will identify top areas of growth across two major areas:  staff count by role and patient volume. The project will study the patterns of highest patient volume and make recommendations on the top 3 shifts and roles to open hiring for.
