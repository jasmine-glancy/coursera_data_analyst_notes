## Purpose: 

*Write a brief description of why this project is happening below. Why is this project happening? What are the goals?*

LVH stakeholders wish to expand their outreach across each state. They are considering the need for feline-specific hospitals and medical staff, and they would like to receive a rough estimate of customers who have dogs or cats in their household. If greater than 30% of families in the state have cats, LVH would like to get a list of 10 locations to prioritize cat-specific practices. If less than 30% of families in the state have cats, LVH would like a cost-estimate analysis of implementing cat-specific entrances and staff for mixed species practices, ranked by state.
