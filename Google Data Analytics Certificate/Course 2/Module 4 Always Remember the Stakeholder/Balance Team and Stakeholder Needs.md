## Summary

Project Managers manage the projects of the entire team. They're in charge of planning and executing the timeline of a project. Stakeholders are people that ​have invested time, interest, ​and resources into the projects that ​you'll be working on as a data analyst. Stakeholders can be the executive team, customer-facing team, or the data science team.

To stay focused across various areas and projects as a data analyst, it helps to ask 3 questions for each of your projects: "Who are your primary and secondary stakeholders?", "Who maintains the data?", And "where do you go for help?"
