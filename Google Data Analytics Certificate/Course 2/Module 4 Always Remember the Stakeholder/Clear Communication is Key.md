## Cues

- To communicate clearly, data analysts ask themselves a series of key questions. One is, “Who is my audience?” What are the others?
- A colleague sent you a question via email nearly two days ago. Finding the answer will involve a few hours of research, and you are too busy to do that today. What’s the best course of action?
- Focusing on stakeholder expectations enables data analysts to achieve what goals?
- A stakeholder asks a data analyst to produce a report very quickly. What are some strategies the analyst can apply to ensure their work delivers useful results?

---

## Summary

To communicate clearly, data analysts ask four questions: Who is my audience? What do they already know? What do they need to know? And how can I communicate effectively with them? When communicating by email, try to aim for a reply within 24-48 hours with a status update, let them know when they can expect your response, and thank them for their patience.

Focusing on stakeholder expectations enables data analysts to understand project objectives, improve communication, and build trust. To ensure their work answers the right questions and delivers useful results, the data analyst should set clear expectations, outline the problem, and reframe the question.
